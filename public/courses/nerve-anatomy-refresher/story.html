Structure

The connective tissue layers

  • Endoneurium — surrounds individual nerve fibres.
  • Perineurium — bundles fibres into fascicles; the blood–nerve barrier.
  • Epineurium — surrounds the whole nerve; the plane of most repairs.
The perineurium, not the epineurium, provides the mechanical strength of a fascicle.
Blood supply

Two systems, one nerve

Peripheral nerves have an extrinsic (segmental, from adjacent vessels) and an intrinsic (longitudinal epineurial and perineurial plexuses) blood supply.

Extensive longitudinal anastomoses allow nerves to be mobilised over considerable distances without ischaemia — within limits.
